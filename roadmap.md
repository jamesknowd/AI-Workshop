# Roadmap

## What this is
A study task list for people learning a language. Each task is tagged with a skill, so learners can see which skill they have been avoiding.

## What Done means
A stranger opens the live site and creates an account with an email and password. They add study tasks, tag each with one of six skills (Reading, Writing, Listening, Speaking, Vocabulary, Grammar), and tick some off as done. They log out, log back in, and find their tasks unchanged. A summary shows how many tasks they completed in each skill over the last 7 days and labels the lowest one "Most avoided." No one else can see their tasks.

## Slices
1. Sign up and log in | done-criteria: (a) On the live site, click Sign up, enter a new email and password, submit; the site shows "Signed in as" followed by that email. (b) Click Log out; the login form appears and the email is no longer shown. (c) Log in with the right email and a wrong password; an error message appears and you stay logged out. (d) Log in, close the tab, reopen the site; it still shows "Signed in as" with your email. | status: DONE
2. Tagged tasks that persist | done-criteria: (a) Logged in, type "Read one news article", choose Reading, click Add; the task appears labeled Reading. (b) Log out and back in; the task is still there with its Reading label. (c) Tick its checkbox and reload; it still shows as done. (d) Sign up as a second account in a private window; none of the first account's tasks appear. | status: DONE
3. Most-avoided skill | done-criteria: (a) Complete one Reading and one Speaking task; the summary lists all six skills, with Reading 1, Speaking 1, and the other four at 0. (b) The four skills at 0 are labeled "Most avoided"; complete one Listening task and reload, and Listening shows 1 without the label. (c) A brand-new account with no completed tasks sees "Finish a task to see your skill balance" instead of counts. | status: ACTIVE
4. Task priority | done-criteria: (a) The Add form has a priority choice with exactly three options, Low, Normal and High, and Normal is picked unless you change it. (b) Each task in the list shows its priority next to its skill label. (c) Add "Learn ten food words" as Vocabulary with High, reload the page; it sits above every Normal and Low task. (d) Add a task with Low, reload; it sits below every Normal and High task. (e) Add two Normal tasks one after the other, reload; they appear in the order you added them, oldest first. (f) Tasks created before this slice show as Normal. (g) Tick a High task done and reload; it stays at its place in the High group. | status: TODO
